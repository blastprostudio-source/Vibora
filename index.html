THANK YOU FOR THE VIBES!

Settings

Appearance

VIBORA uses a clean light colour scheme. Accent shades change gently.

Player animations

Choose how many floating hearts, flowers and sparkles appear while music plays.

Default is 80. 0 turns off floating decorations, up to 100. The animation follows your search: Holi shows colours, hearts and pichkari; Diwali shows crackers and hearts; weddings, birthdays, Eid, Christmas and more have their own.

YouTube API keys

Add your keys one by one. They are saved on this device. VIBORA uses a key until its daily limit is reached or it gives an error, then switches to the next key automatically.

The meter shows the estimated share of the 10,000 daily units (one search costs 100). It starts again from 0% at 12 midnight. A key that Google reports as exhausted is skipped until Google's own reset.

Audio customisation

Fine-tune your listening preferences. Volume, repeat and speed work with the YouTube player. Bass, mid and treble are saved as tone preferences; YouTube's embedded player does not expose its audio stream to browser equalizers.
